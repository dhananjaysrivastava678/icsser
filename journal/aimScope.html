The scope and purpose of ICSSER-IJMRI

Aim & Scope

Aim

The primary aim of ICSSER-IJMRI is to serve as a comprehensive, multidisciplinary outlet that captures the evolving nature of research in the 21st century — where innovation often emerges at the intersection of multiple fields rather than within a single one.

Scope

The journal welcomes submissions from a wide spectrum of disciplines, including but not limited to:

  • Science and Applied Sciences
  • Engineering and Technology
  • Computer Science and Information Technology
  • Management and Commerce
  • Social Sciences and Humanities
  • Education and Pedagogy
  • Law and Governance
  • Health and Allied Sciences
  • Environmental Studies and Sustainability
  • Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Emerging Technologies

Manuscripts that explore interdisciplinary approaches, novel methodologies, applied research, and innovative solutions to contemporary problems are especially encouraged.
